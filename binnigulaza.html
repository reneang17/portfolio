The cultural legacy of Binnigulaza

Stage director Sergio Santamaría is developing a theater production based on the oldest legend of the Zapotec people. This production aims to preserve and celebrate indigenous storytelling through a deeply collaborative effort involving multiple artistic disciplines.

  • Dancers & Choreographers: Translating ancestral narratives into physical movement.
  • Artisans: Crafting authentic textiles and props.
  • Musicians: Designing an immersive soundscape rooted in tradition.
  • Set Designers: Bringing the physical environment of the legend to the stage.
My Role: End-to-End Production

I am responsible for producing the complete audiovisual campaign from the ground up to build interest and showcase this collaborative work. This includes taking photos and recording videos on-site, producing custom audio, writing scripts, editing footage, and adding subtitles.
